## 📌 Problem & Solution

### Problem
When planning weddings, funerals, graduations, birthdays, church functions, or corporate events in Kenya, people rely primarily on personal referrals and repeated phone calls to find caterers. This process is slow, inconsistent, and makes comparing prices, menus, capacity, and service quality difficult.

### Solution
A specialized hospitality marketplace where customers submit one event request and receive matched caterers. Customers can easily filter providers by total budget, estimated cost per guest, location, cuisine, guest capacity, ratings, and real-time availability.

---

## 👥 Target Users
* **Families & Individuals:** Planning weddings, funerals, dowry ceremonies, birthdays, and graduations.
* **Organizations:** Churches, schools, community groups, and corporate event organizers.
* **Hospitality Providers:** Caterers, chefs, buffet-service providers, waiters, and hospitality teams.

---

## ✨ Core Features
* **Event Request Form:** Capture occasion details, date, venue, guest count, cuisine, and budget.
* **Budget Matching Engine:** Calculate cost per guest and match caterers within budget ranges.
* **Provider Profiles:** Display verified menus, food photos, transparent pricing, capacity, and customer reviews.
* **Quotation & Messaging System:** Request custom quotes and communicate directly with providers.
* **Ratings & Verified Reviews:** Build trust through authentic customer feedback.
* **Provider Dashboard:** Allow caterers to manage availability, pricing, inquiries, and menus.

---

## 💡 Budget Matching Example
* **Customer Budget:** KES 150,000 for 200 guests.
* **Calculated Cost per Guest:** KES 750 / guest.
* **App Matching Output:** Filters and displays caterers with packages in the KES 600 – 850 range for exact quotation requests.

---

## 🚀 Recommended Launch Strategy
1. **Research:** Interview event organizers to map user needs.
2. **Onboarding:** Recruit 20–50 verified caterers in Nairobi before public marketing.
3. **Piloting:** Facilitate pilot bookings to refine user experience and provider workflows.
4. **Expansion:** Scale city by city across Kenya after establishing strong regional liquidity.
